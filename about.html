About the yeshiva

Solidifying identity as bnei Torah

Who we are

Bais Medrash Ma'alin Bakodesh offers a unique environment where bachurim can solidify their identity as bnei Torah and develop a sense of ownership over their growth in learning and avodas Hashem.

In an atmosphere permeated with genuine warmth and passion for Torah and Yiddishkeit, our approach focuses on taking achrayus for personal growth and discovering the greatness within. The yeshiva fosters deep personal connections with the rebbeim and a beautiful sense of achdus among the talmidim.

Our mission

Bais Medrash Ma'alin Bakodesh is a Torah institution in Pearl River, New York, where bachurim gather each day for prayer and Torah study. In a warm atmosphere of genuine passion for Torah and Yiddishkeit, our rebbeim guide each bachur to solidify his identity as a ben Torah and take ownership of his growth in learning and avodas Hashem.

The yeshiva today

The yeshiva was founded in 2024 and is an independent institution, not affiliated with any larger organization. Today 20 talmidim learn with us under the guidance of 6 rebbeim.
