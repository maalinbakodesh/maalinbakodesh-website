Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, tracking or advertising tools, and it does not collect personal information on its own pages.

Donations

Online donations are processed by our payment provider, Cardknox, on its secure page. We receive the information needed to record your gift and send a receipt, such as your name, email, address and gift amount. Card details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by us.

How we use information

We use donor information only to process gifts, send receipts and keep our records. We do not sell, rent or share it with others.
