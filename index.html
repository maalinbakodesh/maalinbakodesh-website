Our mission

Bais Medrash Ma'alin Bakodesh is a Torah institution in Pearl River, New York, where bachurim gather each day for prayer and Torah study. In a warm atmosphere of genuine passion for Torah and Yiddishkeit, our rebbeim guide each bachur to solidify his identity as a ben Torah and take ownership of his growth in learning and avodas Hashem.

The program

Designed for the ben aliya, with structure, personal connection and real celebration of growth.

Weekly retzifus seder

A structured framework that helps each bachur build consistency and ownership in his learning.

One-on-one with yungeleit

Personal chavrusas and ongoing shakla vetarya projects that deepen understanding and connection.

Farbrengens & outings

Thursday night farbrengens, Motzaei Shabbos outreach, and outings with rebbeim to celebrate accomplishments.
