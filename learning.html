Our program

Designed for the ben aliya

Every part of the program is built to help a bachur take ownership of his learning and grow in avodas Hashem, with rebbeim who know him personally.

Weekly retzifus seder

A structured framework that helps each bachur build consistency and ownership in his learning, week after week.

One-on-one with yungeleit

Each bachur learns with personal chavrusas and works on ongoing shakla vetarya projects that deepen his understanding and his connection to the learning.

Farbrengens & outings

Thursday night farbrengens, Motzaei Shabbos outreach, and outings with the rebbeim build achdus and celebrate what the talmidim accomplish.

Close bonds with rebbeim

With 6 rebbeim for 20 talmidim, every bachur has a rebbi who knows him, guides him and believes in him.
